bru

Bidirectional Remote Uplink

What is Bru?

Bru (bridge in Norwegian) lets you read and send SMSes from your computer, via your Android phone. It does this by creating an end-to-end encrypted persistent connection to a companion app running as a background service on your phone.

Features

  • Encrypted. Your data is only readable by you.
  • Fully local. No personal data is stored outside of your devices.
  • Hole punching. Works from behind routers and firewalls. No port forwarding or fiddeling with NAT.

How does it work?

Bru leverages Iroh to create a persistent connection between your computer and phone. This project is not related to the second Scottish national drink.

As long as the devices are online, Iroh finds a way to connect them, falling back to using a relay if a direct link is not possible. Your data is in any case still encrypted and not readable by anyone.
